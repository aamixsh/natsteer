# natsteer: plan

MATS project 1 with Isak Etinger. This file is the current plan only. Rewrite it when the plan changes
(do not append history: results live in [docs/presentation.md](docs/presentation.md) and
`docs/results/`, setup in [docs/HANDOFF.md](docs/HANDOFF.md), history in git). Last updated 2026-10-11.

## 1. Goal

Train activation probes for rare safety or misalignment behaviors on gpt-oss-20b with synthetic positives
generated by **steering**, where the steering vector itself defines the behavior (no written-policy grader).
The probe always reads activations recomputed on the generated text **without** intervention.

Success: on held-out, unsteered, natural data, probes trained with steered positives beat probes trained
on (a) natural data at the same label budget, (b) prompted generation and (c) fixed-strength dense
steering, at a fixed false-positive rate (TPR at 1% FPR; AUROC and TPR at 5% FPR secondary, with
per-prompt bootstrap intervals). Secondary: which notion of "natural" and "diverse" predicts the gain.
LASER (Isak's active learning, grader-labeled) stays the natural-data baseline where natural labels are
the expensive resource; synthetic labels from steering are free but noisy.

## 2. Status (2026-10-11)

- **E0 infrastructure** done: hooked bf16 gpt-oss-20b, harmony rendering with a fixed date, segment-aware
  steering, andyrdt SAEs, features, probes, LASER, vLLM grader. Activations reproduce only to about 2%.
- **Refusal testbed, three passes** (`refusal_pipeline.py`, `steer_v1.py` + `steer_v1_extra.py`, `steer_v2.py`;
  numbers in the deck and `docs/results/steer_v*/SUMMARY.md`). Steering induces refusal on harmless prompts
  (CoT-only masks do the work). Delta-attribution finds causal latents: 4 at strengths 2 and 5, **23** once
  strengths 8 and 12 and ranks to 128 are screened (14 at layer 11, 8 at layer 15), a 4-latent bundle 98%;
  activation-difference and random latents 0 of 32. **Synthetic positives do not beat natural data at 1% FPR**
  (10 seeds, prompt-clustered intervals; first 32 CoT tokens, 64 labels: natural 0.66, dense 0.58, prompted 0.53,
  bundles 0.42 to 0.55, 23-latent mix 0.37); mixing synthetic into natural never beats natural; more distinct
  latents did not help. Whole-CoT probes are saturated (0.99).
- **What explains the gap (second and third pass):** topic. Positives steered on the *same prompts* as natural
  refusals (178 WildChat prompts, 12 samples each) match natural data on held-out WildChat refusals (two
  strength-12 latents 0.41, prompted 0.45, natural 0.40; dense direction 0.30, bundle 0.22 still lose), while
  the same generators on harmless prompts reach 0.15 to 0.24. Keeping the 30% most natural-looking synthetic
  positives (cross-fitted discriminator) lifts the union 0.28 to 0.46 and dense 0.48 to 0.56. The gap is largest
  at the layer where natural is best (layer 15: 0.70 vs 0.48; layer 7: 0.60 vs 0.60); layers concatenated do not
  help. Latent steering stays near the natural manifold (reconstruction error 0.11 vs 0.30 dense, natural 0.09)
  but closeness does not predict probe transfer. Prompted generation matches the best steering, so steering has no
  demonstrated edge; as free LASER warm-start labels, synthetic positives help whole-CoT features (0.49 to 0.90
  after 32 natural labels, prompted equally) and not the first-32-token features.
- **E2 at scale and E3** done (`steer_e3.py`, 1.3 h; `docs/results/steer_e3/SUMMARY.md`). E2: for refusal,
  distance from natural (SAE reconstruction error) rises smoothly with yield (Spearman 0.74, 24 latents; strength
  8 and 12 latents run at about 4x the natural error), sycophancy latents stay near natural; a latent's data is more
  useful when its yield is high (0.76), not when it stays near natural (0.37, n.s.). E3: best-of-8 selection by a
  discriminator trained on 128 natural refusals adds about 6 points (label-free selection hurts); a per-token cap
  at the natural 90th-percentile reconstruction error hurts latent data and helps the dense direction only at
  strength 3; every synthetic arm stays below natural on harmless prompts (best 0.58 vs 0.70), while on naturally
  refused prompts capped latents and prompted text match natural. Naturalness is a second-order lever after topic.
- **Sycophancy testbed** done: baseline 0.20 sycophantic answers; the CoT direction at layer 11 reaches 64%
  (E0 proxy +2 to +5 points); 39 of 40 attributed latents survive fresh items (best bundle 74%); random latents
  reached 38% in the first screen. Probes detect it poorly (natural: TPR at 1% FPR 0.23 at 256 labels, 0.46 at
  2,048; AUROC 0.85 at 256) and synthetic-only plateaus at 0.22 (39-latent mix, dense 0.13). **A random steering
  direction gives the same probe quality**, CoT-length matching changes nothing: with an exact answer-key label
  the vector is only a sampler.
- **Intent label** (harmful intent of the user turn, graded against a researcher-written policy): pools and
  features built, grading blocked on the policy. Optional track.

## 4. Design decisions

| Decision | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Model | gpt-oss-20b (24 layers, d=2880, MoE), bf16 with dequantized experts for hooks and gradients | Reasoning model, public SAEs, known layers (15 prompt, 19 CoT) from arXiv 2507.03167 |
| SAEs | andyrdt/saes-gpt-oss-20b BatchTopK, 131k latents, k=64 or 128, resid_post layers 15 and 19 used | CoT variance explained 0.74 (L15) and 0.83 (L19); the Goodfire L15 SAE is too weak (0.48) |
| Behavior definition | The steering vector, from contrast sets (refusal) or oracle-labeled pairs (sycophancy); labels read from the model's own output | No policy to write; evaluation labels independent of the steering vector |
| Steering | Addition at resid_post of one layer (L15 end-of-prompt direction, L11 CoT direction, attributed latents); strengths as multiples of the raw difference of means or of a latent's mean active value | Window is narrow: coefficient 3 breaks all-position generation |
| Generation | vLLM with eager hooks for all-position and prompt-only masks (`natsteer.vllm_steer`); HF `Steerer` for CoT-only and final-only | vLLM about 50x faster; the HF path supports segment masks |
| Latent choice | Contrastive delta-attribution (OpenAI, Dec 2025) on same-prompt behavior and no-behavior samples; compare with activation difference and random latents | Attribution ranking finds causal latents, activation difference does not |
| Probe | Logistic regression on pooled clean activations (`cot_mean`, `cot_first32`, `final_mean`) at layers 11, 15, 19 | Cheap; prompt-reading probes cannot use steered data because steering leaves prompt tokens unchanged |
| Naturalness | Three levels: activation (distance to natural, reported against the 2% noise floor), token (some text reproduces the behavior), distribution (discriminator AUC) | Level 3 should predict probe transfer; levels 1 and 2 explain it |
| Diversity | Text compression and distinct-n, plus coverage of natural positives in activation space | Raw steering success was anti-correlated with downstream accuracy and diversity falls with strength (arXiv 2605.28664) |
| Rendering | Every prompt through `harmony.render` with `config.FIXED_DATE` | The stock template stamps today's date and shifts activations |

## 5. Later experiments (kept from the original proposal)

- **Reachability (E2; distances at scale done in `steer_e3.py`, GCG-style search not done and lower priority, since
  closeness to natural did not predict usefulness).** Distance of steered activations to the natural set: autoencoder reconstruction
  distance, nearest neighbor in a position-matched natural bank, and a GCG-style suffix search
  (Soft-GCG, arXiv 2607.08883) with an L2 loss against a target activation. Report against the 2% noise
  floor. Tests whether effect size against distance is bimodal, a spectrum or flat.
- **Naturalness-constrained generators (E3; done: best-of-K discriminator selection and per-token reconstruction
  cap, see status; co-activation bundling not tried).** Latent bundling from co-activation structure on natural
  positives; dynamic per-token strength keeping reconstruction error under a natural quantile or keeping a
  natural-vs-steered discriminator confused. Drop a variant with no interpretable signal after a pilot.
- **Active-learning comparison (E4).** Arms: natural-only LASER, prompted generation, fixed steering,
  bundling, dynamic steering; same label budgets (100 to 2,000), 5 seeds; controls: probe trained on
  steered activations themselves, random-direction steering.
- **Prompt recovery (E5).** For strong settings, look for natural text that elicits comparable behavior (CoT
  swap, few-shot, paraphrase search). Failure is evidence, not proof.
- **Stage B security subset** (HarmBench, CyberSecEval, CySecBench) once the method works on refusal and
  sycophancy.

## 6. Findings that constrain the design

- Recomputation noise: the same prefix run alone and inside a longer sequence differs by about 2% (median)
  at layer 15, from MoE routing flips; per-token distance claims must beat this floor.
- Reasoning effort "low" gives CoTs of 5 to 30 tokens; use medium.
- gpt-oss refuses harmful prompts with a fixed sentence ("I'm sorry, but I can't help with that."), so
  refusal is stated in the text and whole-CoT or final-answer probes saturate at AUROC 0.999; separation
  needs 1% FPR, few labels or the first CoT tokens.
- A single refusal direction is distributed on gpt-oss and trace-dependent (arXiv 2609.14759, a preprint);
  CoT-only steering reaches 97 to 100% refusal, final-answer-only 12 to 16%.
- vLLM hooks need eager mode and no prefix caching; FlashInfer fails on sm_120 (Triton attention is forced).
- The safeguard grader refused about 2% of harmful-looking inputs even under a harmless toy policy; a real
  grading run needs a retry or fallback.

## 7. Sources that matter

LASER (Etinger, OpenAI alignment blog, 2026-10-06); SAE latent attribution (Dupre la Tour and Mossing, Dec 2025);
arXiv 2507.03167 (refusal in reasoning models, gpt-oss-20b layers 15 and 19); 2605.26772 (CoT disrupts
steering; steered-CoT swap keeps about half the effect, one 8B model); 2605.28664 (steering-generated
data for text classifiers: about a quarter of settings beat prompting); 2511.17408 (off-policy probe data;
refusal text-evident, sycophancy and deception worst); 2511.21399 (models detect steering); 2604.09839
(steered activations are non-surjective); 2607.08883 (Soft-GCG). Literature read second-hand through a
summarizer; no published test of steering-generated data for activation probes was found.

## 8. Risks

- Steering label is not the behavior: success depends on strength; mitigate with the verifier and an audit
  slice, and train on verified positives only.
- Circularity: a probe trained on steering-defined labels may rediscover the steering direction; keep the
  natural-trained control and a topic-matched held-out set.
- Diversity collapse with strength; report text and activation diversity in every table.
- Small natural sets give wide intervals at 1% FPR; use per-prompt bootstrap intervals.
- Hazard: assistant-side harmful-compliance data needs Isak's handling rules; until then positives are
  over-refusals on harmless prompts and generations stay in `data/` (never released).
