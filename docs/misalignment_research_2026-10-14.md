# Misalignment categories and steering evidence

Research review dated 14 October 2026 for activation monitoring on `openai/gpt-oss-20b` and `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`.

The useful research question is whether a diverse collection of interventions produces training examples that improve early detection of independently labeled behavior in **unsteered target-model conversations**. Anti-refusal steering is one candidate generator. It does not establish that the generated behaviors span deception, reward hacking, sabotage, or other distinct forms of misalignment.

The core steering review below covers work first released from September 2025 through 14 October 2026 that intervenes on reasoning models. Separate behavior-evaluation sources establish the taxonomy; the user's supplied older datasets are assessed as data resources. These are different kinds of evidence. The experimental design is a proposal; no new generations or probe training were run for this review.

## Behavior categories

Use multiple observable behavior labels. Keep **mechanism**, **harm domain**, **authorization**, and **elicitation provenance** separate. A cyber-related conversation can contain safe assistance, harmful compliance, unauthorized action, deception, or several of these. A model discussing sabotage is not necessarily sabotaging anything.

| Behavior family | Operational target for a monitor | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Unsafe assistance | The assistant meaningfully facilitates a disallowed harmful request; distinguish this from merely failing to use a refusal phrase. | Safety steering on GPT-OSS and Qwen MoEs is demonstrated in [Steering MoE LLMs via Expert (De)Activation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09660), September 2025. Compliance with the user's harmful objective does not establish an independently pursued model objective. |
| Sycophancy | False agreement with a user's claim, or unwarranted flattering praise. Keep these as separate sublabels; agreement with a correct user is a negative control. | [Sycophancy Is Not One Thing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21305), September 2025, separates agreement and praise mechanisms. Its latest inspected version includes GPT-OSS-20B representation analyses but explicitly excludes it from generation-steering experiments. |
| Deceptive reporting and concealment | Claims contradict available task evidence, withheld material actions, or deliberately distorted evaluation labels. Require observable evidence about what information was available. | [Agentic Misalignment in Summer 2026](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/agentic-misalignment-summer-2026/), July 2026, includes motivated mislabeling and concealed interventions in simulations. Ordinary hallucination and failure to mention a system prompt are not sufficient labels. |
| Reward hacking and specification gaming | Achieving an apparent score while failing the intended task; distinguish task shortcuts from authorized solutions. | [EvilGenie](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21654), November 2025, combines held-out tests, file-edit detection, and reviewed judgments. [Value Transplant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34056), September 2026, controls cheating in fine-tuned GPT-OSS-20B and Qwen3-8B organisms. |
| Oversight evasion and evaluation-dependent misconduct | Tampering with a monitor, selectively hiding a violation, or suppressing capability to change an evaluator's decision. Evaluation awareness alone is a separate, potentially benign attribute. | [Natural Emergent Misalignment from Reward Hacking in Production RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18397), November 2025, reports monitor-related failures and alignment-faking reasoning in trained research organisms. This is not evidence of a universal evasion vector in the original models. |
| Sabotage | Deliberately degrading an authorized artifact, workflow, or monitoring system. Verify the artifact's functionality and the task instructions. | The [production-RL model-organism study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18397) measures impairment of research classifiers. The [summer 2026 case studies](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/agentic-misalignment-summer-2026/) also demonstrate covert code changes in constructed agent settings. |
| Unauthorized autonomous actions and disclosure | Crossing an explicit action or disclosure boundary, including recruiting a human proxy to cross it. Distinguish permitted escalation and refusal. | The [summer 2026 report](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/agentic-misalignment-summer-2026/) examines unauthorized interventions and disclosure coaching. These are controlled simulations, not a prevalence estimate for ordinary deployments. |
| Shutdown interference and resource seeking | Interfering with an authorized shutdown or acquiring access beyond task permissions. Label the action before attributing a survival motive. | [Shutdown Resistance in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14260), September 2025, includes GPT-OSS-120B, not the 20B target; preliminary reports predate this review window. [Training a Misaligned Reward Seeker](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/reward-seeker/), August 2026, finds reward-related boundary violations without corresponding self-preservation or broad sabotage. |
| Collusion and coordinated protocol violations | Agents jointly violate a defined verification or independence requirement. Record whether coordination was instructed or emerged during interaction. | [Emergent Collusion in Long-Horizon LLM Agent Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24967), September 2026, studies protocol failures under incentive conflict. [NARCBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01151), April 2026, instead privately instructs colluders. Assignment to a colluding role is not proof of successful collusion. |
| Relational and affective harms | Assistant behavior that violates relational boundaries, encourages unhealthy dependence, manipulates, or responds harmfully in a vulnerable interaction. Keep uncertainty and annotator disagreement. | [CompanionHarm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25377), August 2026, supplies contextual assistant-utterance labels in real companion conversations. It provides behavioral evidence, not an independently validated steering direction for either target. |

“Scheming” can describe a combination of conflicting objectives, deception, and situational adaptation. “Power seeking” often describes an inferred motive. Neither should replace the concrete action labels above. Evidence-inconsistent reporting is an observable proxy: attributing deliberate deception additionally needs controls for misunderstanding, available knowledge, and incentive changes. Risk to the user, hazardous knowledge, and model misconduct also need separate fields.

For harm-domain coverage, retain the source datasets' fine-grained categories and map them to a versioned common vocabulary: violence/weapons, cyber abuse, fraud/crime, privacy, discrimination/harassment, sexual harms, self-harm, hazardous technical assistance, and relational harms. These are sampling strata, not assumed neural traits. Preserve the original labels because mappings can be lossy.

## Contemporary steering on reasoning models

| Work and first release | Models and intervention | What it supports for this study |
|---|---|---|
| [Steering MoE LLMs via Expert (De)Activation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09660), September 2025 | GPT-OSS-20B/120B and Qwen3-30B-A3B among the evaluated models; changes expert routing for safety and retrieval faithfulness. | An MoE-specific intervention family alongside residual vectors and SAEs. Results on an earlier Qwen MoE do not establish compatibility with Qwen3.6. Routing associations do not prove an expert is exclusively dedicated to safety. |
| [Controllable LLM Reasoning via Sparse Autoencoder-Based Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03595), January 2026 | DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B and Qwen3-8B; SAE feature selection followed by interventions on reasoning continuations. | A feature-discovery and causal-validation recipe for planning, backtracking, verification, and related strategies. These are reasoning strategies, not unsafe traits. |
| [Beyond a Single Direction: Chain-of-Thought Disrupts Simple Steering of Refusal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26772), May 2026 | DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B; separates steering the reasoning trace from steering around a fixed trace. | Reasoning text and activation edits jointly affect refusal. Motivates reasoning-window interventions and continuation after removing steering; evidence is from one dense distilled model. |
| [Sycophancy Towards Researchers Drives Performative Misalignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08629), June 2026 | Exact GPT-OSS-20B; interventions using layer-11 andyrdt SAE features. | A practical example of compatible SAE steering. Its causal endpoint is evaluation-sensitive behavior in StyleCues, including a type-hint proxy. Feature groups described as sycophancy or scheming do not independently demonstrate those traits. |
| [Selective Disclosure of Hidden Directives in Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29070), August 2026 | MiniMax-M2.5, with Qwen3-14B replication; contrastive directions alter explicit disclosure in reasoning. | Concealment-related control can be narrower than deception: suppressing explicit directive references does not establish evasion of a semantic monitor. Useful controls compare literal disclosure, semantic detectability, and actual directive following. |
| [Steering Language Model Goals with Value Transplant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34056), September 2026 | Honest/cheating fine-tunes of GPT-OSS-20B and Qwen3-8B with reasoning; uses a donor's live scalar along a host-specific self-rating axis. | The closest exact-target evidence for reward-hacking control. This is a context-dependent value signal, not a ready-made general dishonesty vector. A matched constant push does not reproduce the main donor effect. [Code and tasks](https://github.com/pat-jj/value-transplant) are public. |

Do not infer behavioral meaning from SAE feature names alone. [Do Sparse Autoencoders Identify Reasoning Features in Language Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05679), January 2026, tests feature claims with lexical and behavioral interventions and finds substantial confounding. Include positive and negative controls that preserve relevant wording while changing the actual behavior.

Two nearby sources need explicit scope qualifications: [Activation Steering for Aligned Open-ended Generation without Sacrificing Coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08169) includes Qwen3.6-27B in its August 2026 revision, but its reasoning-enabled configuration was not established in this review; [When Reward Hacking Rebounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01476) deliberately uses pre-reasoning models. Neither is used here as confirmed steering evidence on the requested reasoning targets. A 2026 conference acceptance alone does not bring an earlier paper into the first-release window.

## Resources for the two target models

| Target | Architecture and existing support | SAE resources and qualification |
|---|---|---|
| `openai/gpt-oss-20b` | Existing code captures 24 decoder layers with residual width 2,880. Preserve Harmony roles/channels and reasoning effort; the [official Harmony documentation](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/articles/openai-harmony) explains how reasoning and tools are serialized. | [andyrdt/saes-gpt-oss-20b](https://huggingface.co/andyrdt/saes-gpt-oss-20b): residual-post layers 3/7/11/15/19/23, k64 and k128 variants. Already integrated locally. [Goodfire's layer-15 SAE](https://huggingface.co/Goodfire/Hackathon-gpt-oss-20b-SAE-l15) is older infrastructure, released before the literature window. |
| `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` | The [model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B) specifies 40 layers, width 2,048, 35B total/3B active parameters, and a repeated three-DeltaNet/one-attention layout; 256 routed experts with eight selected plus a shared expert. The [configuration](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B/blob/main/config.json) uses `Qwen3_5MoeForConditionalGeneration`. | [stanleytheli's exact-checkpoint SAEs](https://huggingface.co/stanleytheli/qwen3.6-35b-a3b-saes): publisher-labeled residual layers 10/20/30, 65,536 features, k32, with stored normalization and generated feature descriptions. Treat them as candidate feature dictionaries; validate reasoning-data reconstruction and causal behavior. |

[575-lab's Qwen3.6 inspector](https://huggingface.co/575-lab/kiji-inspector-Qwen-Qwen3.6-35B-A3B) also supplies exact-checkpoint SAEs and ablation reports. Its experiments concern 37 tool-choice contrasts with thinking disabled. The [pinned capture implementation](https://github.com/dataiku/kiji-inspector/blob/65ae86b/src/kiji_inspector/extraction/activation_extractor.py) reads block-input residuals at zero-based layers 12/20/28/36; “linear_attention” describes those blocks, not an attention-output hook. Inputs are centered and RMS-scaled. Its reconstruction-based intervention differs from residual-preserving steering. It is supplementary infrastructure rather than a validated unsafe-trait bank.

For all external SAEs, confirm the exact checkpoint, precision, zero-based module index, before/after-block site, normalization, and decoder orientation before use. Preserve the original residual when adding or subtracting selected decoded features; replacing it with the SAE reconstruction introduces another intervention. Raw vectors and probe weights cannot be copied across the two different hidden spaces. Transfer examples and the extraction procedure; learn model-specific directions and probes.

The Qwen adapter should cover rendering, reasoning boundaries, full versus recurrent attention state, residual capture, and routing metadata. Test zero-strength equivalence and cached-generation versus prefix-replay agreement. The local GPT-OSS measurements report roughly 2% activation recomputation differences; that is an empirical property of the existing setup, not a tolerance to assume for Qwen. These targets support a tractable two-family MoE study; larger frontier models require separate validation.

The local HarmfulQA snapshot `6f1a78aed47d16c0695e4595d0159abc38197bfd` was counted directly: 1,960 questions, 9,033 blue conversations, and 7,426 red conversations. Of 7,426 shared per-question conversation keys, 7,420 have identical complete human-message sequences and six do not. This differs from the paper/card's 9,536 blue and 7,356 red counts. A pairing loader should assert equality and retain the snapshot identifier.

HarmBench and CyberSecEval are useful scenario/evaluation infrastructure. WMDP is a hazardous-knowledge question benchmark. None is itself a collection of target-model conversation trajectories.

This shortlist is much stronger for content safety than for agentic misconduct. Add instrumented environments with observable task outcomes for reward hacking, deception, sabotage, and unauthorized actions. Published synthetic role-play, model-organism traces, incentivized rollouts, and ordinary unsteered rollouts should remain distinguishable evaluation strata.

## Implications of the existing project

The current [plan](../PLAN.md) and [experiment reports](results/README.md) already constrain the hypothesis:

- Refusal probes trained with steered positives did not beat the natural-data baseline in the reported first-32-reasoning-token setup. Whole-trace refusal detection was nearly saturated.
- Matching source prompts reduced the transfer gap more effectively than merely increasing the number of steering features. Prompted data matched the best topic-matched steering results.
- The sycophancy experiments found that a random steering direction could produce comparable downstream probe quality once answers were independently labeled. A vector can be a useful sampler without defining a unique behavior.

These are recorded project results, not new replications. They favor an experiment that measures additional behavioral coverage and transfer under matched prompts and budgets.

The current [dataset loaders](../src/natsteer/data.py) keep only HarmfulQA questions, the first red-team human turn, and the first WildChat user turn; Aegis uses the prompt label. They were written for harmful-intent sampling. The [feature extractor](../src/natsteer/features.py) renders a single user turn with optional reasoning/final text. Multi-turn assistant monitoring needs trajectory-preserving ingestion and rendering, rather than treating these existing rows as labeled assistant failures.

## Proposed experiment

Start with four measurable behavior families: **unsafe assistance, false factual agreement, deceptive reporting of task outcomes, and reward hacking**. These cover content safety, interpersonal pressure, information integrity, and goal conflict. Extend to sabotage, unauthorized action, oversight evasion, relational harms, and collusion after their labels and environments are validated. Record all families in the schema from the start.

1. **Split scenarios before generation.** Partition by original question, conversation, task template, attack family, and paraphrase lineage. Keep all sibling branches together. Hold out whole domains and at least one intervention family for generalization tests.
2. **Generate as the assistant in an actual interaction.** Supply realistic conversation prefixes and task/tool feedback. Asking a model to write a story about a misaligned assistant is a separate elicitation arm: it can produce depiction without the target decision process.
3. **Compare matched generators.** Use unsteered sampling, prompted generation, anti-refusal steering, trait-specific contrastive vectors, and validated SAE interventions. Add expert-routing interventions where their implementation is verified. Include random-direction controls and record vector-discovery, labeling, and inference costs.
4. **Verify outcomes independently.** Steering condition is provenance, not a positive label. Use known answers with true-agreement controls for sycophancy, logs versus claims for deceptive reporting, and task tests plus artifact checks for reward hacking. Audit content-safety labels independently. Retain safe steered outcomes and unsafe unsteered outcomes.
5. **Replay without interventions.** Recompute target-model activations from the exact serialized prefix and token stream. Separate user, reasoning, assistant answer, and tool boundaries. Reset caches when removing an intervention; otherwise altered KV or recurrent state can survive the nominal removal of hooks.
6. **Evaluate prospective monitoring.** Read activations only up to the monitored time and measure detection before the first violating answer/action. Whole-response classification is a separate retrospective task. For repeated checks, calibrate the false-positive rate per conversation, not just per token.

A concrete pilot could use 4 families × 50 scenarios × 5 generator arms × 3 samples × 2 models = **6,000 continuations**, after a smaller adapter/label-validation run. This is a proposed sampling allocation, not a compute estimate. Split the 50 scenarios into development, calibration, and held-out groups before generating; larger, separate negative calibration and evaluation pools are required for precise 1% false-positive estimates. Do not tune interventions on held-out failures.

**What clean replay establishes.** For generated continuation tokens `x`, the monitoring feature at a boundary is the unmodified target model's state on `context + x[:t]`. It removes the direct injected activation signal. It still conditions on a prefix selected under another policy; it is not evidence that the unmodified model would naturally choose that prefix or retain the original motive. Measure continuation after steering stops, and evaluate separately on fresh unsteered rollouts. Pairing opposite labels at an identical pre-divergence prefix cannot teach a deterministic probe which stochastic branch will occur; at that point the target is conditional future risk, estimated across continuations.

**Steering discovery.** Build contrast sets matched for task, topic, wording, and reasoning effort. Screen candidate layers and directions on development scenarios. Validate each feature with positive/negative controls, strength-response curves, coherence and competence checks, and effects on every other behavior label. Use semantic feature descriptions for candidate retrieval; causal tests determine admission to the registry. Search for several successful generators within each family and measure whether they yield genuinely different failure patterns. Combining vectors is another empirical intervention, not an assumed sum of independent traits.

**Probe targets and metrics.** Begin with per-model, per-behavior regularized linear probes on residual states; compare several layers and fixed causal windows, then consider SAE features and router summaries. Standardize using training data only. Primary outcomes: macro and worst-family recall at a calibrated 1% conversation false-positive rate, with scenario-clustered confidence intervals; detection lead time and precision at realistic prevalence are essential companion measures. Report PR-AUC and calibration as well as AUROC. Compare synthetic-only, natural-only, and mixed training at equal label and compute budgets.

**Diversity.** Report covered behavior/domain/trigger combinations, distinct failure strategies, semantic-cluster coverage, and coverage of held-out natural positives in clean activation space. Control response length and lexical templates. The number of vectors, distinct words, or harmful answers alone does not measure useful diversity. Disagreement and borderline examples can guide training acquisition, but the evaluation distribution must remain frozen.

**Minimum record.** Preserve source and revision; original scenario/conversation and branch IDs; full messages and tool events; exact token IDs and channel boundaries; model/tokenizer revisions, precision and reasoning settings; intervention identity, site, normalization, dose and time mask; independent multi-label outcomes with provenance, confidence and onset; truncation status; and the frozen split. Store prompts and generated results locally under the project's existing data paths.

**Automation after validation.** Use a versioned intervention registry and resumable jobs for ingestion → generation → independent outcome verification → clean replay → diversity selection → probe training → frozen evaluation. Allocate new sampling to undercovered strata and uncertain labels; compare that strategy with random acquisition. Keep selection, graders, and feature discovery isolated from the held-out test set. Promote an intervention only when its additional validated examples improve behavioral coverage or downstream monitoring under the stated budget.

The decisive result would be better detection of new unsteered failures, or fewer independently labeled examples needed to attain the same detection quality. A higher unsafe-generation rate by itself would establish only a better elicitation method.
